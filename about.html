American Courier Express is a Tampa Bay courier. The work is rush, express, same-day, and next-day, plus standing routes. Coverage is the bay and Lakeland — Tampa, St. Petersburg, Clearwater, Brandon, Lutz, Wesley Chapel, Plant City, Lakeland, Bradenton, Sarasota — and jobs booked beyond that, including statewide runs.

Desks sit in South Tampa, North Tampa, downtown, and Mulberry. Customers reach the desk at +1 612-649-9537 or americancourierexpress@my.com.

Accounts on this desk are separate from any outside portal. Sign in to see your runs, or look up a run number without an account.

How a run is booked

  1. Name the window

    Rush, express, same day, or next day. If it is a repeating stop, say route.

  2. Give both addresses and the load

    Pickup, drop, what it is, and the weight if you know it. For a filing, add the court and the deadline.

  3. Dispatch prices it

    Window, weight, distance, and area. Tallahassee corporate filing is the exception: those fees are posted.

  4. One driver finishes it

    The vehicle that collects the shipment is the vehicle that delivers it.
